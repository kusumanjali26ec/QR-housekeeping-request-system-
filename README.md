# QR-housekeeping-request-system-

📱 QR-Based Housekeeping Request System

📌 About the Project

The QR-Based Housekeeping Request System is a simple digital solution designed to make it easier for students and staff to report cleaning and maintenance problems on campus.

Instead of searching for a housekeeping worker or verbally communicating the problem, a person can simply scan a QR code, select the type of problem, and submit a request.

---

❗ Problem Statement

In classrooms, corridors, and other campus areas, students may find it difficult to communicate cleaning or maintenance problems to housekeeping staff quickly.

Common problems include:

- 🧹 Unclean classrooms
- 🗑️ Overflowing dustbins
- 💧 Water spills
- 🚪 Cleaning required in corridors
- 🔧 Minor maintenance issues

There is a need for a simple and quick way to communicate these problems.

---

💡 Proposed Solution

We propose a QR-Based Housekeeping Request System.

A QR code can be placed in classrooms, corridors, and other important areas.

When a student scans the QR code, a request form opens. The student can select the problem and submit the request.

🔄 How It Works

Student notices a problem
↓
Scans the QR code
↓
Request form opens
↓
Selects the problem
↓
Submits the request
↓
Housekeeping team receives the request
↓
Problem is addressed

---

🎯 Objectives

- Make housekeeping requests easier.
- Reduce communication delays.
- Provide a simple digital reporting method.
- Help housekeeping staff identify the location of the problem.
- Improve cleanliness and maintenance on campus.

---

🛠️ Technologies / Tools

- QR Code
- Google Forms or an online form
- Smartphone
- Internet connection
- Spreadsheet/database for storing requests

---

👥 Users

Students and Staff

They can scan the QR code and submit a request.

Housekeeping Team

They can receive and respond to submitted requests.

---

🌟 Benefits

- Quick and easy reporting
- Reduces verbal communication problems
- Saves time
- Helps identify the location of the issue
- Creates a record of submitted requests
- Can be expanded to other campus services

---

🔮 Future Improvements

📷 Prototype

The prototype consists of QR codes placed at selected locations such as classrooms and corridors. Each QR code can be connected to a request form for reporting housekeeping problems.

---

📚 Design Thinking Process

1. Empathize

Understand the difficulties faced by students and housekeeping staff while communicating cleaning requests.

2. Define

Students need a simple and fast method to report cleaning and maintenance problems.

3. Ideate

Develop a QR-based system that allows users to report problems using their smartphones.

4. Prototype

Create QR codes and connect them to an online request form.

5. Test

Place the QR codes in selected campus areas and collect feedback from users.

---

🚀 Conclusion

The QR-Based Housekeeping Request System provides a simple and practical way to improve communication between students and housekeeping staff. It uses easily available technology to make reporting problems faster, more organized, and more convenient.
